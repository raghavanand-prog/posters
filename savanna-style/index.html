⚠️ This Was a Quishing Simulation

Scanning QR codes can redirect you to convincing fake sites that harvest your login credentials under the guise of discounts, rewards, or in-store perks — this is called "quishing" (QR code phishing).

Red flags to look for:

  • Unsolicited QR codes — on posters, flyers, or stickers you didn't expect
  • Urgency-based discounts ("today only," "limited time," steep percentages off)
  • Being asked to log in or register before any transaction has taken place
  • Mismatched or unfamiliar URLs that don't match the brand you expected
